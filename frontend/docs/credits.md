# Credits

All 3D in the Life Road is procedural (three.js primitives), built in this repo. No third-party
models, textures or sounds are used yet.

## Placeholders that need real art

- **Character**: box-and-sphere figure. Wants a low-poly older figure with idle, walk and celebrate clips.
- **Zone 1**: tent (4-sided cone), signpost, fire. Fine as low-poly, but a kit tent would read better.
- **Zone 2**: cottage, well and fence from boxes and cones.
- **Zones 3–7**: wireframe boxes in `components/world/zones/ghosts.ts`.

When real assets arrive, use one CC0 kit throughout (Kenney or Quaternius), exported as GLB with
meshopt or Draco compression and vertex colours or a single atlas no larger than 1024 px, processed
with gltf-transform. List each pack here with its licence and URL.
